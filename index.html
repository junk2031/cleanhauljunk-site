Questions people ask us

How do you price from a photo?

We estimate how much of our Sprinter van your items will fill and quote the matching fixed price. If what's there on the day matches the photos, that's the price. If there's more, we show you and agree on it before loading.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. If the items are outside, in a garage, or someone can let us in, we can do the job and you pay after.

Do I need to carry anything out?

No. We pick up from wherever the items are, including basements, attics, upstairs bedrooms and backyards, and sweep up after.

How soon can you come?

Often the same or next day, depending on the schedule. Book online and pick your day and arrival window.

How do I pay?

You pay after the job is done, once everything is loaded and you've seen the van. We accept Zelle and cash. We never ask for payment in advance.

What happens to my stuff?

Usable furniture and household items are donated when possible, metal and electronics are recycled, and the rest goes to a licensed disposal facility.
